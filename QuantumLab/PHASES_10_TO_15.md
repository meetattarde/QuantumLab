# Phases 10–15: Integrated professional-lab upgrade

## Delivered

1. **Phase 10 — Reproducible runs:** timestamped run records with input parameters and scalar result metrics.
2. **Phase 11 — Analysis:** peak, range, area and density statistics for experiment outputs.
3. **Phase 12 — Guided comparisons:** curated presets for key physical contrasts, including threshold and barrier-width demonstrations.
4. **Phase 13 — Digital lab notebook:** versioned JSON session save/load, designed to remain readable and portable.
5. **Phase 14 — Usability:** record history, active-run metrics, session controls, and clear empty/history states in the experiment interface.
6. **Phase 15 — Quality:** session/metrics/preset tests and documentation of the portable session format.

## Scope note

Recorded metrics describe the plotted educational model and are not experimental uncertainty estimates. A saved session stores settings and summaries—not full raw graph arrays—so files remain small and readable.
